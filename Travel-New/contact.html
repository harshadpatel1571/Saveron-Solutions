Clear Answers Before You Depart

Everything you need to know about our nature retreats, corporate offsites, and in-trip ground support.

How are boutique eco-resorts and nature stays selected?

Every sanctuary property is personally vetted by our operations team for hygiene, scenery, peaceful surroundings, prompt service, and environmental sensitivity.

Are holiday itineraries fully customizable?

Yes, 100%. All package templates on our site are initial blueprints. You can add extra days in Munnar, upgrade to a luxury treehouse, or customize private naturalist treks.

What happens if a flight is canceled or delayed during my trip?

Our 24×7 Active-Trip Desk immediately re-books alternative flights, alerts the destination pickup chauffeur, and notifies your retreat check-in desk for seamless coordination.

What payment methods and invoicing formats are supported?

We support corporate bank wire transfers (NEFT/RTGS), credit cards, and UPI. All corporate travel bookings receive 100% GST-compliant tax invoices with detailed expense breakdowns.
