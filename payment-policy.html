Payment Policy

This is starter website content and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before the website begins accepting bookings, payments or personal data.

Payment requests

Pay only against an official quotation or invoice and through a payment method confirmed by SAVERON. Verify account details through an official contact before transferring funds.

Booking status

Payment does not guarantee supplier availability. A booking is confirmed only when SAVERON issues written confirmation after payment and supplier acceptance.

Schedules and currencies

Deposits, balance dates, taxes, gateway costs and currency considerations will be stated in the proposal. Missed deadlines may result in release or repricing.

Receipts and discrepancies

Retain transaction evidence. Report discrepancies promptly to accounts@saveronsolutions.com.
