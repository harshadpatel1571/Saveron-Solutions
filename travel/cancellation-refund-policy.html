Cancellation and Refund Policy

This is starter website content and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before the website begins accepting bookings, payments or personal data.

Cancellation requests

Requests should be submitted in writing. The effective time is when SAVERON acknowledges receipt during applicable business operations.

Charges

Cancellation and modification charges vary by airline, hotel, transport provider, experience, season and notice period. Non-refundable or special-rate components may be charged in full.

Refund processing

Eligible refunds are processed after the relevant supplier returns funds and applicable charges are reconciled. Banking and supplier processing time may extend timelines; an estimate should be stated in the confirmed booking terms.

Schedule or weather changes

Alternative arrangements remain subject to availability and may create additional cost. SAVERON does not control airline, hotel, transport-provider, weather or government decisions.

Force majeure

Refunds and credits for events beyond reasonable control depend on supplier terms and recovered amounts.
